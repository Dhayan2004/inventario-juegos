## Pre-strategy
Read product-marketing context (product-marketing.md), then assess:
1. Business context — product, audience, conversion goals
2. Opportunity — search patterns, realistic page volume, demand distribution
3. Competition — who ranks now, their structure, viability of beating them

## Five core principles
1. **Unique value per page** — differentiated content, not variable swaps.
2. **Data hierarchy**: Proprietary > Product-derived > User-generated > Licensed > Public.
3. **Subfolders, not subdomains** — subfolders consolidate domain authority.
4. **Genuine search-intent match** — each page must truly answer its query.
5. **Quality over quantity** — 100 great pages beat 10,000 thin ones.

## Implementation (5 phases)
1. **Keyword pattern research** — find repeating structure + variables; validate
   demand (aggregate volume, distribution, trends).
2. **Data requirements** — sources (first-party, licensed, public); plan updates.
3. **Template design** — unique intros (not swaps); conditional content by data.
4. **Internal linking** — hub-and-spoke (category hub + individual pages);
   breadcrumbs; no orphans.
5. **Indexation strategy** — prioritize high-volume patterns; noindex thin
   variations; separate sitemaps by type.

## Pre-launch checklist
- Content: unique value per page · answers intent · readable/useful
- Technical: unique titles/metas · heading hierarchy · schema · page speed
- Architecture: connected to site structure · related pages linked · no orphans
- Indexation: in XML sitemap · crawlable · no conflicting noindex

## Post-launch monitoring
Track: indexation rate, rankings, traffic, engagement, conversion rate.
Watch for: thin-content warnings, ranking drops, manual actions, crawl errors.

## Common failure patterns
1. Thin content (city-name swaps only) 2. Keyword cannibalization 3. Over-
generation without demand 4. Poor/stale data 5. UX neglect (built for Google,
not users).
