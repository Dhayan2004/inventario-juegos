# /lanzamiento — Go-To-Market Plan

Diseña el plan táctico de lanzamiento: positioning, canales por prioridad, launch sequence. Sub-análisis individual de `el-crisol` (paso 7 de 7 — último antes del dashboard).

## Ejecución

Lee `.claude/skills/el-crisol/SKILL.md` y ejecuta **solo el paso `lanzamiento`** del pipeline.

## Análisis

Genera `GTM-PLAN-<nombre>.md` con:

1. **Positioning statement** — formato April Dunford:
   - For [target customer]
   - Who [problem/desire]
   - [Product] is a [category]
   - That [key benefit]
   - Unlike [alternative], we [differentiator]
2. **Messaging matrix** — copy diferenciado por segmento (early adopter / pragmatist / skeptic).
3. **Channel prioritization (top 3-5):**
   - Por canal: CAC estimado, time-to-payback, scalability ceiling, dependencies
   - Recomendación: 1 channel principal + 2 supporting (foco gana — diluir mata)
4. **Launch sequence (8-12 semanas):**
   - Semana -4 a -1: pre-launch (waitlist, build-in-public, partnerships, PR seeding)
   - Semana 0: launch day (Product Hunt, HN, LinkedIn, comunidades específicas)
   - Semana +1 a +4: amplification (case studies, content, paid ads si CAC validado)
   - Semana +5 a +8: optimization loop (cohort analysis, conversion funnel debugging)
5. **Launch KPIs** — signups, activation rate, week-1 retention, paid conversion.
6. **Risk register** — top 3 cosas que pueden salir mal + planes de contingencia.

## Pre-flight

Idealmente después de `/brujula` (Channels sección 7), `/rivales` (positioning differentiated), `/precio` (pricing public).
